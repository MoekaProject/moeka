# Code of conduct

Moeka welcomes participants with different backgrounds, languages, experience levels, and perspectives. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, and other project-hosted conversations.

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code, not the person contributing them.
- Be constructive and specific. Allow room for questions, mistakes, and disagreement.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or make personal attacks on others.
- Do not post unwanted sexual content, spam, or other people's private information.
- Respect requests to stop an unwelcome interaction.

Maintainers may moderate content or limit participation to keep project spaces constructive. This short policy does not establish a dedicated response team or a guaranteed response schedule.

If you encounter a concern, contact a repository maintainer through an available private channel. If no private channel is listed, ask for one without posting sensitive details. GitHub's reporting and blocking tools are also available. Do not share private reports or identifying information publicly without permission.
