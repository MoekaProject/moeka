# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Give constructive feedback on ideas and code, welcome questions and disagreement, and respect requests to end an interaction. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual content, spam, and disclosure of others' private information are prohibited.

This policy applies to project-hosted conversations. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation. Report concerns through an available private maintainer channel, or request one without sharing sensitive details; GitHub's reporting and blocking tools are also available.
